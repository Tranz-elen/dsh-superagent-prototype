- The case export records two timestamps per `CaseRecord`: `createdAt`, when the Ticket was created, and the optional `endedAt`, when it ended; both are ISO 8601 UTC strings copied from `ticket.createdAt` and `ticket.ended.at`.
- It applies to every exported ended-Ticket line in `cases.jsonl` (written on `ticket.ended` plus startup catch-up) whenever ordering, durations or time-to-outcome are derived from cases.
- Failure mode: using `createdAt` where `endedAt` is needed measures request time rather than completion, so time-to-outcome and end ordering are wrong; `endedAt` is also absent until the Ticket ends.
- Reviewer check: in `src/app/cases.ts` confirm `CaseRecord.createdAt` is required and `CaseRecord.endedAt` optional, that `caseOf()` copies `ticket.createdAt` and sets `endedAt` only from `ticket.ended.at`, and in a `cases.jsonl` line that `endedAt` is not earlier than `createdAt`.
- Source: `src/app/cases.ts` — `CaseRecord` interface (the `createdAt`/`endedAt` fields) and `caseOf()` (their construction).
