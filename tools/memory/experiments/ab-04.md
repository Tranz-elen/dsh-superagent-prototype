- A failure candidate is evidence, not a rule: one repeated tool-failure signature with its case ids, outcomes, symptom and a suggested owner scope, waiting for a human-written cause, probe and action.
- It becomes a candidate when `buildFailureCandidates` groups `trace.failures` by signature and the signature's occurrence count reaches `FAILURE_MIN_CASES` (2), after quarantined failures and ability verdicts are skipped.
- Failure mode: occurrences are counted, not distinct cases, so two identical failures in one ticket can qualify a candidate backed by a single case; only `validateFailureCandidate` enforces two cases.
- Reviewer check: confirm the candidate cites at least two distinct case ids, and reject a one-case candidate before writing its cause, probe or action.
- Source: `tools/memory/failure-candidates.mjs`, `buildFailureCandidates(cases, options)`.
