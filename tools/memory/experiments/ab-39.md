- `cases.jsonl` records one line per ended Ticket, next to the state database; it may also carry the `promptRevision` its worker ran under.
- It applies only when a ticket ends (`ticket.ended`, plus catch-up on startup); work that never became a Ticket is recorded in `decisions.jsonl`, not here.
- Failure mode: treating decisions as tickets — an `ignore`, refused `stop` or `unavailable` is a Decision, not a Ticket, so counting those rows as cases inflates the corpus and applies ticket rules to work that never ran.
- Reviewer check: open `tools/memory/README.md` Data section and confirm two separate rows — `cases.jsonl` per ended Ticket on `ticket.ended`/catch-up, `decisions.jsonl` per Decision that never became a Ticket on `decision.recorded`/catch-up.
