- Human review is required because the playbook is behavior-shaping text injected into the worker prompt: review is the only gate between distilled advice and injection, and the prompt revision history is the review trail.
- The review happens after distillation (`pnpm memory:distill`) and before a person pastes only high-confidence rules into the `playbook` team section and saves the `PUT prompts` revision with `baseRevision`.
- Skipping review lets an unevidenced or untrusted rule reach the prompt — for example a rule with fewer than three real case ids — so every later worker follows wrong advice until someone re-reviews the revision.
- Reviewer check: for each rule in `tools/memory/rules.json`, confirm `evidence` lists at least three case ids present in `dist/clusters.json`, then replay its `check` on those cases (for `edit-read-first`: after the retry, confirm the edit succeeded and the file contains the intended change).
